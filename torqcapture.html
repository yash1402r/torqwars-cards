TorqCapture

Call of Duty objective modes — territory control with RC cars · Standalone game

Two teams fight over glowing zones. Drive in, out-number them, hold the ground — the ring flips to your colour and ticks points. It's the only true team-vs-team game in the set, and the zones flipping colour on the overhead cam is a live map of who's winning.

The layout + the tech

How you hold a zone

  • Occupy = hold. Your car sits in the lit ring; the team with more cars inside controls it.
  • Deny = shove. Rivals drive in to contest — you body-check them out. The bumper is the weapon.
  • Capture = hold the majority for a ~5s timer → the ring flips your colour and ticks points, even after you roll off to fight elsewhere, until they take it back.
  • Scored by tech, not a marshal — a tag on each car + a zone reader; the ring lights up on capture.

The playlist

One floor, a COD-style rotation — swap the rules + zone layout in minutes.

Domination

3 zones A/B/C — hold to tick, own 2 of 3 = the tug-of-war. The default.

Hotzone

One lit zone that relocates every 60–90s → the whole pack scrambles.

The Hill

One central zone, hold it longest. The quick round + the final.

Siege Point

Capture locks the zone for N seconds → assault vs defend. Advanced.

Courier

The legacy flag-run, kept as one mode (absorbed Capture the Zone).
