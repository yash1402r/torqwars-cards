TorqSiege

Castle siege — build & smash with RC cars · Standalone event #2

Teams race a clock to build a castle from foam and cardboard. Then the opposing drivers smash it to pieces. The single best reel in the whole idea bank — a car through a wall, blocks flying, slow-mo collapse.

The loop

A never-stops clock. The pressure never resets to calm — that's the rush.

1. Build · 5–8 min2. Siege · 90 s3. Reinforce · 2–3 min4. Siege 2 · 90 s5. Final
Defend the core. Every castle protects a central block — a balloon, a light, a mascot. Siege scoring = topple the core, not just chip the wall. Build-and-smash becomes offense vs defense with a clean win condition.

Build phase

Snap-and-stack only — no glue, no tools, anyone builds fast. The skill is where you build, not how. A shrinking clock + a central material scramble = the rush.

Grapple Hook

Snag a wall section, drive away, topple it. The cinematic raid.

Gated on one test. Light rubber-tyred buggies hit soft — so the wall must be engineered to collapse bigger than the hit. A one-afternoon collapse test decides whether TorqSiege is a hero event or needs a rethink. Everything here is designed around proving that first.
